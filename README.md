# My Mini Tour Guide: Paris 🇫🇷

Welcome to my *mini tour guide!* This guide will help me plan my trip to **Paris**, discover famous places, and try *delicious* French food.

## Things to Pack
*Comfortable* walking shoes
- A camera or phone
- A backpack
- A water bottle
- Sunglasses
- A map of Paris
## **Iconic** French Food
- Croissants
- French baguettes
- Macarons
- Crêpes
- French cheese
## **Top 3 Places to Visit**
1. Eiffel Tower – A famous landmark with an amazing view of the city.
2. Louvre Museum – A museum where I can see famous artworks like the Mona Lisa.
3. Notre-Dame Cathedral – A historic cathedral with beautiful architecture.
## **My Itinerary**
1. Day 1: Visit the Eiffel Tower and take pictures.
2. Day 2: Explore the Louvre Museum and try French pastries.
3. Day 3: Visit Notre-Dame Cathedral and walk along the Seine River.
